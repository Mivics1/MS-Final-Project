## 11. Word fields

The baseline's table of contents is **static text, not a Word TOC field** (the document
contains no `fldChar`, `instrText` or `PAGEREF` markers), so it cannot refresh itself and
went stale when the figures were resized. Rather than replace it with placeholder text, the
page numbers were recomputed from the rendered PDF and rewritten in place, then re-exported
and re-checked until stable. **All 13 chapter-level entries were verified against the printed
page folios.**

The List of Figures and List of Tables carry captions without page numbers in the baseline;
that presentation is unchanged.

**No manual Word field update is required.** Opening the document and pressing F9 is
harmless but unnecessary.

## 13. Unresolved items

1. **Student ID** — omitted by choice (see above).
2. **Exploratory audit** — the screening estimates are not independently held out; the
   dissertation says so. A fresh audit under a different seed would be needed before any
   deployment claim.
3. **Layout** — Figure 6.2 is 15.2 cm tall and cannot fit beneath Table 6.3, leaving white
   space on that page. This follows from the requested larger figures and the instruction
   that readability takes priority over page count.
4. **Wholesale rebuild deliberately not run** — `build_final_dissertation.py` would
   regenerate the document from the older, less cautious wording and overwrite the aligned
   baseline's refinements. The finalisation therefore edits a copy of the baseline
   (`finalise_validated_docx.py`), consistent with the "do not perform a wholesale rewrite"
   rule. The generator script is retained for provenance but is no longer the source of the
   final document; Appendix A names the scripts that actually produce it.
